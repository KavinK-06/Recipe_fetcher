Privacy Policy

Rasoi · Last updated 18 June 2026

Note: This page is a starting point tailored to how Rasoi works. We recommend a review by legal counsel before you rely on it.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Rasoi mobile app (“Rasoi”, “we”, “us”), operated by Kavin, collects, why, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up we collect your email address, and any name or profile photo you provide. Authentication is handled by our provider Clerk.
  • Recipe content. Links you import (e.g. YouTube or recipe URLs) and the recipes we generate from them, including ingredients, steps, and any collections you create.
  • Photos you submit. If you use the calorie-scan feature, the dish photo you choose is sent for analysis to estimate nutrition. Photos are processed for that request and are not used to identify you.
  • Purchase information. When you buy a one-time purchase or credits, the app store (Apple or Google) processes the payment and tells us the purchase status. We do not receive your card details.
  • Basic usage & device data. Limited technical data needed to operate the app reliably (e.g. import counts and credit balances).

How we use your information

  • To create, store, and display your recipes and collections.
  • To provide AI features such as recipe extraction and calorie estimation.
  • To process purchases and track your remaining import/scan credits.
  • To secure your account and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to support requests.

Service providers we share data with

We use trusted processors to run the app. They only receive what they need to perform their function:

  • Clerk — authentication and account management.
  • Supabase — database and file storage for your recipes and images.
  • AI processing providers (via OpenRouter) — to extract recipes and estimate nutrition from the content you submit.
  • Transcript/import providers — to fetch publicly available video transcripts and web page content for the links you import.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — to process in-app purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account inside the app. Deletion removes your recipes, collections, and sign-in identity. Some records may persist briefly in backups before being overwritten.

Children

Rasoi is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can exercise most of these in-app, or contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
