Delete Your Account

Rasoi · Last updated 25 June 2026

This page explains how to delete your Rasoi account (operated by Kavin) and the data associated with it. You can do this yourself inside the app, or by emailing us.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the Rasoi app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom-right).
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm when prompted.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email kavinninja2006@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your sign-in identity and account profile (email address, name, profile photo).
  • All recipes you imported or created, and your collections.
  • Photos you uploaded for recipe import or calorie scanning.
  • Your credit balances and entitlement (plan) records.

What may be kept

For legal, tax, and accounting reasons we may retain a minimal record of past purchases/transactions (for example, that a one-time purchase was made) for as long as the law requires. This record does not include your card details, which are handled by Google Play and never stored by us. Residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about deleting your account? Email kavinninja2006@gmail.com.
